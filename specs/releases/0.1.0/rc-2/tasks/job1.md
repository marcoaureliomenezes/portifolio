The root-level `navProjects` key is deleted from the 3 files and `types/content.ts:354-355`; the dead comment at `types/content.ts:317` is reworded.
- **Header (J1.T9).** `Header` takes no props. Links: Resume to `/`, Projects to `/projetos`, `NavLink` active state (`aria-current="page"`; Projects current on `/projetos*`). Theme toggle and language selector keep their components and `track()` events. The phone header, both rows, stays at or under 80 px (T-LAY-01). No avatar (`layout.spec.ts:53-58`), no menu button, no scroll-state class.
- **SiteLayout (J1.T10).** Pathless layout route in `App.tsx`: `<Header/>`, `<main id="main-content">` padded by `--header-h`, `<Outlet/>`. `/`, `/projetos*` (the project shell nests inside it) and `*` all sit under it. `page_view` stays in `App`'s mount effect, unchanged and not copied into the layout. `NotFound` loses its own `<main>`; `Portfolio.tsx` loses only `pt-16 md:pt-[72px]`. The 4 project templates and `ProjectsIndexPage` still hold their own `<main>` until Job 6; the layout test therefore stubs the outlet, and "no nested main" closes in Job 6.
- **first paint (J1.T13).** The inline script sets `dark` unless `localStorage.theme === 'light'`, the same rule as `useTheme.readInitialTheme`, which is unchanged. `<html lang="pt-BR">` stays as the no-JS default; `og:locale` is untouched.
- **Tokens (J1.T7).** `theme.extend.fontSize` adds the steps: `label` 12 mono, `meta` 13 mono, `body` 16, `item` 17/600, `hero` 20/700, `title` 32/700 and `title-sm` 28/700 (phones).
